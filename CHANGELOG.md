# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (first version)
- Offline local library (MediaStore + picked folders), albums, artists, genres, folders, favorites, recents
- Media3 playback service with MediaSession, notification, lock-screen and Bluetooth controls
- Queue management with drag reorder, restore of the last queue (paused)
- Playlists with M3U/M3U8 import and export
- Equalizer with presets and custom presets, bass enhancement, loudness boost, balance
- Fade between songs, sleep timer, playback speed
- Purple glass UI (dark and light), reduced motion, haptics setting
- Local-privacy notice and licence notices inside the app
